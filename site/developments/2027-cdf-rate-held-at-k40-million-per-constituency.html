2027 CDF rate held at K40 million per constituency

6 November 2026 · Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development (MLGRD)

The Constituency Development Fund stays at K40 million per constituency in 2027. The rate came during the 2027 budget presentation on Friday 6 November: the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development published the figure at 16:29, quoting Finance and National Planning Minister Dr Situmbeko Musokotwane: "In the 2027 fiscal year, each Constituency to get K40 Million."

That is the same rate as 2026, when CDF rose to K40 million per constituency and the 156 constituencies shared K6.24 billion. Whether the rate would hold was one of the live questions of this budget. In October the Finance Minister warned that next year's CDF would be lower than first budgeted as the Treasury contained overspending, State House then said the K40 million stood, and the Treasury's budget hearings projected the fund rising to about K9.0 billion, the arithmetic of K40 million across the new 226-constituency map.

What the announcement does not yet settle is the basis and the total. The ministry's post states the per-constituency rate only. It does not say whether the rate applies to the current 156 constituencies or the new 226, and it gives no total envelope. Those figures sit in the budget speech text and the Estimates, due from the Ministry of Finance after the presentation. This tracker will record the basis and the total from those documents when they appear, and the 2027 line will join the budget tables then.

For councils and communities, a held rate means 2027 planning starts from a known number rather than a cut. What the rate adds up to nationally still depends on the basis, and what it delivers will be judged here the same way as every year before it: by what is released, what is used, and what gets built.

Source: Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development (MLGRD) — 2027 CDF rate announcement during the 2027 budget presentation (official Facebook post, 6 Nov 2026, 16:29 CAT)
