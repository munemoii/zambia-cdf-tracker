The future

Model the 2027 budget

The 2027 rate is set in the national budget, and the delimitation question hangs over it: 156 constituencies today, 226 gazetted. Try the combinations and see what each one costs.

—

For reference: 2026 is K40 million × 156 = K6.24 billion. Holding the rate at K40m across 226 seats costs K9.04bn; holding the total at K6.24bn across 226 seats cuts the rate to about K27.6m each.

Sources

  • Component split — up to 5% administration is taken first; the remaining 95% is split 60% community projects / 20% empowerment (40% grants, 60% loans) / 20% bursaries, which works out to 57% / 19% / 19% of the total allocation — CDF Guidelines, Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development.
