How one constituency's money is split

For a K40.0m allocation (2026), under the CDF Guidelines.

Up to 5% covers administration. The remaining 95% is divided 60 / 20 / 20. Inside empowerment, 40% is grants and 60% is soft loans.

Sources

  • Allocation growth 2021–2026 and delivered results — Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development, “CDF increase to K40 million: what it means for Zambia's communities”, and national budget reporting of the 2026 budget (K40m per constituency, ≈K6.24bn total).
  • Component split — up to 5% administration is taken first; the remaining 95% is split 60% community projects / 20% empowerment (40% grants, 60% loans) / 20% bursaries, which works out to 57% / 19% / 19% of the total allocation — CDF Guidelines, Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development.
  • Province constituency counts (156 total, before the 2026 delimitation) — Electoral Commission of Zambia, 2026 Delimitation of Constituencies — Province Summary.
  • 2025 release and utilisation figures (K5.26bn released, ≈94%; 86% utilisation; 2,985 community projects worth K2.9bn) — as reported nationally for the 2025 CDF; treat as reported figures, not audited project-level records.
