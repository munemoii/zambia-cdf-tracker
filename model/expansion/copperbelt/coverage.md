# Copperbelt Province CDF expansion — coverage report

Collected: 30 October 2026 (research pass, published sources only; no Facebook used — council social posts were reached only through third-party mirrors and every row sourced that way is in `held.csv`).
Output: `projects.csv` — **93 project rows** across 19 of the 22 Copperbelt constituencies, traced to official publications in `sources.csv` (115 sources captured in total; 6 are aggregate/gap sources with no rows, listed at the end). `held.csv` — **141 rows** that must not go live without Caleb's review.

## How this pass differs from the Lusaka pilot — read first

1. **Almost no Copperbelt council publishes a consolidated CDF project list.** Lusaka's backbone was council approved-lists, a council newsletter and CDFC minutes. On the Copperbelt, only **two** such lists were found: Masaiti Town Council's 2022 CDF commissioned-projects list (all attributed to Kafulafuta) and Lufwanyama Town Council's Approved 2025 CDF Community Projects list. Everywhere else the systematic official record is **ZPPA e-GP tender notices** (`eprocure.zppa.org.zm`) — official government publications that name the project, constituency, usually the ward, and often the CDF funding year in the title. Rows from notices opened on the ZPPA site itself are in `projects.csv` with status `approved`.
2. **The aggregator rule applied consistently:** where a project is known *only* from a tender aggregator (Tender Impulse, dgMarket, GlobalTenders, BidDetail, TendersOnTime, Bidding Source, Great Zambia Jobs) and the ZPPA original was not opened, the row is **held** — *except* the two Kalulushi 2025 CDF notices (Twalubuka laboratory, Kabaso Bridge), where the aggregator carries the full official notice text verbatim including tender reference and "under 2025 CDF" wording; those two are in `projects.csv` and flagged here so Caleb can demote them if he prefers the stricter rule.
3. **Notices that never mention CDF are held, even on the ZPPA site itself** (Chowa Health Post in Mpongwe; DAPP classroom in Luanshya; five Kitwe notices; the Chingola "misc" notices). A council tender in the CDF era is probably CDF-funded, but "probably" is not plain — they wait for review.
4. **Two judgement calls standardised across researchers:** drainage works are categorised `sanitation`; desk procurements are categorised `equipment`.
5. **ZPPA notices publish no per-project budgets and no winning contractors** (the K500 figure in notices is the bid-document fee and was never recorded). Per-project money on the Copperbelt comes only from council lists/posts and the Auditor-General reports.
6. **Spelling preserved as published**, including ZPPA title typos ("Drainange", "Constituecy") in source titles and one project name ("Construction of 200m Drainange at St Augustine").

## Per-constituency coverage

### Ndola City Council (4 constituencies)

**Ndola Central — 2 main, 34 held.** The thinnest official record relative to its size. Main: the Dola Hill School 1×3 CRB (Dag Ward, ZPPA 2025) and the Kakupepa Culvert (Kaniki Ward, council post, completed). The held pile is dominated by one document: Lusaka Times' 24 Aug 2024 enumeration of the MP's mid-term review — ~20 completed and ~9 in-progress projects named by ward (classroom blocks at Chibolele, bridges in Dag Hammarskjöld/Nkwazi/Kansenshi, George Clinic, Kansenshi Secondary laboratory, Twapia Market, ablution blocks, Ndibu Road street lighting, the K1,000,000 Ndola Teaching Hospital car park, 2,140 desks, a tractor loader backhoe…). It reads like the constituency's own project list but is a single news report with no amounts, so it waits for review. Four more Ndola Central tenders are aggregator-only (Kanini bridge, Twapia Primary completion, Mapalo ablution, Twapia 8 km roads). The Lesa Wa Luse 1×3 block is held on a **status conflict**: the council's progress post reports construction under way with commissioning awaiting water works, while the Lusaka Times list counts a Lesa Waluse block as completed. Note: this Kakupepa/Lesa Wa Luse story is **syndicated onto Chingola Municipal Council's news page as well** — it is Ndola Central (the post names Ndola Central's MP and the Dag Hammerskjold/Kaniki wards), and no Chingola rows were taken from it. Aggregates not rowed: the council reports 200+ community projects completed 2022–2025 (Open Zambia, Jun 2026) and K150m+ channelled 2022–2024 (Excel feature).

**Chifubu — 5 main, 0 held.** Best Ndola coverage. The 2022 ZPPA lot tender (NCC/DES/PSU/CDF/CH/W/04/22/22-08, 5 lots) gives Twatasha Clinic, the Chiti Mkulu Market ablution block + water reticulation, and the Mutambala clinic — **only 3 of the 5 lot names could be captured**; lots 2 and one other are a known gap. Council news posts add the completed Twatasha Clinic (Pamodzi Ward, commissioned with maternity wing and fence — rowed once, sourced to the council post) and the Pine Groove Market Shelter + ablution block (Kawama Ward, commissioned).

**Kabushi — 3 main, 8 held.** Main: Lubuto Market shelter, Chipande Road paving (Mukuba Ward), Dambo Combined School roof rehabilitation (2024). Held: three further lots of the multi-lot 2025 tender (Masala Skills CRB, New Masala Clinic maternity wing, Kabushi Market ablution block) — aggregators attach the shared reference CWD/02/25-21 to different lots inconsistently, so lot-level attribution needs the original tender document; Referendum Road paving (aggregator-only); and four example projects from the council-PR magazine feature (1,000 desks, motor grader, water reticulation improvement, contribution towards Ndola Teaching Hospital road paving). Standing gap: MLGRD approved **11 Kabushi community projects worth K13,326,968 under the 2022 CDF** (reported Aug 2022) — the 11 projects are not named anywhere found.

**Bwana Mkubwa — 1 main, 1 held.** Main: Lupili Ward road grading/re-gravelling (ZPPA; tendered twice — Sep and Nov 2025 notices — rowed once from the later notice). Held: the Katondo Water Reticulation Phase 1 (Munkulungwe Ward) from the magazine feature. Pre-window items excluded by design: the 2018 Kantolomba Clinic tender and the MP's 2019 statement listing 2017 CDF projects (Kantolomba and Mwenye clinics, placenta pits at Mushili and Kaloko clinics, Kansengu Market toilets, Jacaranda Police Post) — outside the tracker's 2021–2026 window, noted here only.

Its two headline stories name no projects: the handover of **27 completed CDF projects (K29,129,780.61 combined)** names only the Kwacha grader/tipper, and the **19 contracts (K7,143,954.32)** story gives only the 6/5/5/2/1 constituency split. ### Rural councils — Mpongwe, Masaiti, Lufwanyama

**Mpongwe — 0 main, 7 held. The emptiest record on the Copperbelt.** Mpongwe Town Council's own CDF Projects page exists and promises a searchable database (ward/category/status/progress fields) — it returns **"No Projects Found"**: zero project cards. Homepage CDF items are generic features naming no project, ward or amount. Searches of the council site, ZANIS, MLGRD and the National Assembly surfaced no list, PDF, newsletter or contract-signing list. Everything named is held: Kaloko Primary staff house/ablution/water (aggregator-only), the Mpongwe South Boarding industrial borehole under the CDF Disaster Component (aggregator-only, with a self-conflicting title), a 20,000-litre water bowser (aggregator-only), the Chowa Health Post works (ZPPA original — but CDF nowhere stated), the Muyambe Health Post award (ZPPA award notice with contractor LADSTAR Enterprises Ltd and K1,214,662.93 but **no project title and no CDF mention**), and two FY2023 Auditor-General observations (Bwandaila 1×3 CRB; Luswishi Health Centre staff house — Luswishi is also the ward of Lufwanyama's Chantete project; the audit places this staff house under Mpongwe council).

**Masaiti — 3 main, 7 held; Kafulafuta — 12 main, 4 held (+ joint rows).** Masaiti Town Council's 17 Feb 2024 commissioned-projects post is one of only two true lists in this expansion — and it attributes **all 11 items to Kafulafuta Constituency** even though the projects sit in Masaiti-district wards (the source's attribution was taken exactly as stated). Two data-quality flags inside that list: the Kambowa Combined School project appears **twice at the identical K698,157.02** (second listing held as an apparent duplicate), and the Mapalo ablution block and Mapalo Health Post construction carry the **identical K199,570.35** (both rowed as published — they are differently-named projects; the identical figure may be a source error). Masaiti's main rows beyond the list are single ZPPA notices: Myria Mokola maternity annex (Mishikishi Ward), the water bowser, and the Chola Community School 1×3 CRB — the last is explicitly **2026 CDF**, the only 2026 project row in the expansion. Audit-only items held: Nkulumashiba and Chankute Secondary CRBs (2022; Chankute's first contract was terminated over defects), the 16-borehole Kashitu/Shimibanga programme (K712,000, Shashi Drilling — completed except one dried-up borehole whose contract was terminated), the Kafulafuta River–Mupapa Bridge road opening (the K30,150 recorded is a questioned labour payment, not a contract sum), the Lupiya Primary borehole (equipping outstanding; a K150,000 2025 CDF top-up for a pump/tank was later approved), and an unnamed school's failed solar installation (contract terminated, contractor Manchila Engineering). Three more 2022 bid-invite items (Kamafwasa Zone CRBs; Lwampesa and Matete staff house/health post pairs; Mutaba Road rehabilitation for Kafulafuta) survive only as an aggregator's verbatim reproduction of the council's invitation to bid.

**Lufwanyama — 18 main, 4 held.** The best-covered constituency in the expansion. The council's **Approved 2025 CDF Community Projects** post (10 Feb 2025) lists 13 projects (dormitories, boreholes, classroom blocks, Milulu Health Post completion, hospital solar phase 1, desks, a low-bed, the Somap Shop restocking…); it publishes the **K17,657,020.74 programme total only**, so no per-project budgets were taken, and it gives almost no wards (Chantete/Luswishi is the exception).
