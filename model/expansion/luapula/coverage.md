# Luapula Province — CDF project-list coverage

Collected 2026-10-30 following the Lusaka pilot method: only rows taken plainly
from official published lists (council/government publications, or a National
Assembly report that itemises projects) go in `projects.csv`. Single news
reports, pre-approval lists, ambiguous items and unattributable rows go in
`held.csv` with a reason. Facebook was not used.

**Totals: 129 rows in projects.csv, 171 rows in held.csv, 21 sources.**

## Results by constituency

### Bahati (Mansa Municipal Council) — 0 main, 2 held
No community-project list is published. Mansa Municipal Council publishes
approved grants and bursary lists for Bahati (e.g. "Approved Bahati 2025
Grants", "Bahati Skill Development Bursary 2025") — those are empowerment/
bursary beneficiary lists containing personal phone numbers, so they were not
imported; only community projects are tracked. Held: the Chisunka Combined
School project (Mutuna Ward, K2,424,429.94, council news) and the Kampalala
Primary School classroom block (news report, cost only "over K700,000").
A council news item on K21,336,466 of 2024 CDF earth-moving equipment covers
Bahati and Mansa Central together and is held under no constituency.

### Bangweulu (Samfya Town Council) — 20 main, 0 held
One clean official list: "Bangweulu Constituency 2025 Community Projects -
CDF (Approved)" (PDF on samfyacouncil.gov.zm, uploaded Nov 2025): 20 projects
with ward and status (all "Approved"). No amounts, contractors or completion
percentages are published. Item 19's name is garbled in the PDF itself
("Veronica Chikonda Clinic Furniture, walk lamps and walk away") and is
recorded as printed.

### Chembe (Chembe Town Council) — 13 main, 0 held
Chembe Town Council's own site published the full list of 13 CDF projects
commissioned by the Luapula Province Permanent Secretary on 23 October 2025
(total value K11,362,954.96; no per-project amounts, no wards). Chembe
District is a single-constituency district, so all 13 are attributed to
Chembe Constituency. Status: completed (commissioned).

### Chiengi (Chienge Town Council) — 0 main, 2 held
The council's CDF web page publishes application forms and secondary-school
bursary lists only — no community project list was found. The only named
community projects anywhere are Nakwisa and Mulolwa Community Schools,
mentioned in a Radio Christian Voice news report of a CDFC visit; held.

### Chifunabuli (Chifunabuli Town Council) — 0 main, 1 held
The council reported on 15 May 2025 that 42 CDF community projects had been
implemented in the district, but it has never published the itemised list —
the gap itself is the record. One project is named in a separate council
news item (Mweshilungu Health Post, Kasongole Ward) and is held. Other
council news items (Kakasa Skills Training Centre rehabilitation, ECE
centres) do not state CDF funding and were not recorded.

### Chipili (Chipili Town Council) — 0 main, 9 held
No council-published project list was found (the council site carries CDF
notices and a CDF Tracker link but no itemised community projects). A Zambia
Monitor report of the Permanent Secretary's June 2025 handover says 13
projects worth K17.2 million were commissioned but names only nine; all nine
are held because the report does not reproduce an official list and gives no
per-project costs, wards or funding year.

### Kawambwa (Kawambwa Town Council) — 9 main, 1 held
The council's 2025 approvals announcement itemises Kawambwa Central's
projects (recorded under the ECZ name "Kawambwa", as the council calls the
constituency Kawambwa Central). No per-project amounts or wards. Held: the
Ntulo Road rehabilitation (news report; K2.5 million funded jointly by CDF
and the council's own capital fund).

### Luapula (Lunga Town Council) — 0 main, 107 held
Lunga is the best-documented council in the province — and both of its
published lists fail the "plainly stated status" test, so all 107 rows are
held pending a status decision:
- "Luapula Constituency 2024 CDF Project List Submissions per Ward" (56
  projects, by ward, with sector and location): these are Ward Development
  Committee **submissions** for the 2024 CDF — proposals, not stated as
  approved or implemented.
- "Deferred Community Projects under 2025 CDF Allocation" (51 projects, by
  ward): an official list of projects **deferred** in 2025 because the
 2025 allocation was channelled to completing stalled projects.
If the tracker later accepts "submitted"/"deferred" statuses, Luapula
Constituency immediately gains the province's deepest ward-level dataset.

### Mambilima (Mwense Town Council) — 26 main, 6 held (+5 shared catalogue rows held unattributed)
See Mwense below. Held separately: six 2025 approvals reported only by
ZANIS (chief's palace, police cell, mortuary shelter, two water schemes,
retainers' motorbike).

### Mansa Central (Mansa Municipal Council) — 0 main, 22 held
The council published the Mansa Central CDF Committee minutes of 30 August
2022, which contain a 16-project table (project, sector, location, ward and
engineer's estimate, VAT inclusive) — but the committee only **recommended**
the projects and resolved to submit them to the Ministry for approval, so
all 16 are held, plus 3 rows from a "Replacement for James Nsonga" table and
2 rows from a further small table in the same minutes whose context could
not be verified, and one news single (Donald Chushi School). No later
consolidated Mansa Central list was found; the council otherwise publishes
grants/bursary lists and individual news items.

### Milenge (Milenge Town Council) — 9 main, 4 held
Milenge Town Council publishes newsletters and a CDF Tracker page but no
itemised community project list. The itemised source is the National
Assembly's report of the 8 April 2025 commissioning: eight projects with
exact per-project costs (four health, four education), plus medical
equipment for Milenge District Hospital (K1,216,000), all recorded as
completed. The same report's empowerment grants (K2,478,178.35 to 72
beneficiaries) are a programme total, not projects, and were not recorded.
Held: four projects named only in council news items (Butute Maternity
Annex; Katena, Chituba and Kamfanka site-visit items).

### Mwansabombwe (Mwansabombwe Town Council) — 6 main, 7 held
No consolidated list. The council's press statement of 13 June 2024 itemises
the CDF projects handed over that day with costs — six rows are plain
(grader, Chipita Rural Health Post, Mabel Shaw water reticulation, 500
desks, Mubanda and Nachimpunu water kiosks). One item (Mumbolo Secondary
School classroom block) is held because its K344,823.76 cost sits in a
clause that also mentions a girls' secondary school. A January 2025 council
post commissions "13 CDF projects" but names only generic equipment and
infrastructure types, so no rows were taken from it. Six further projects
from a May 2026 ZANIS commissioning report are held (two have no named
location; two maternity annexes share one combined cost).

### Mwense (Mwense Town Council) — 25 main, 0 held (plus shared rows — see Mambilima)
The province's biggest single source: Mwense Town Council's official **CDF
Projects Catalogue** (PDF, April 2026), which states that 113 community
projects have been implemented across Mwense and Mambilima constituencies
since 2022 at K82.4 million. The catalogue is a photo-caption document; its
text layer yields about 56 itemised entries, of which 51 could be attributed
plainly to one constituency (25 Mwense, 26 Mambilima) and are in
projects.csv. Five entries are held unattributed: the Sesa RHP entry (no
locality or constituency printed), the Lukwesa Primary CRB and Kashiba
water scheme (locality printed, constituency not), the 40 solar streetlights
(headed "Mwense/Kalanga", spanning both) and the CDF 2023 earth-moving
equipment package (all wards, both constituencies). One catalogue entry —
the Kalanga Fish Market Shelter — is explicitly a **World Bank 2025 Capital
Project**, not CDF, and was excluded entirely. The catalogue prints no
per-project amounts (only the K82.4 million programme total), so all budgets
are blank. Status mapping: "Commissioned"/"Completed"/"Delivered" →
completed; the three entries marked "not Commissioned" → ongoing.
One attribution note: the Chilengwe Rural Health Post entry prints
"Chachacha constituency" (Chachacha is a ward); it is recorded under Mwense
because the same catalogue's companion entry for the same facility reads
"Chachacha – Mwense Constituency". The catalogue's locality names are
recorded in the ward column as printed; it does not distinguish wards from
villages.

### Nchelenge (Nchelenge Town Council) — 10 main, 0 held
One clean official list: "List of Approved Projects for the Year 2026 in
Nchelenge Constituency" (PDF on nchelengecouncil.gov.zm): 10 projects with
ward for each. Items 1–2 (insurance and servicing of the CDF monitoring
vehicle and earth-moving equipment) are recorded with component/category
"administration"; the rest are community projects. No amounts published.

### Pambashe (Kawambwa Town Council) — 11 main, 4 held
The same Kawambwa Town Council 2025 approvals announcement itemises 11
Pambashe projects (equipment, two health facilities with water schemes,
medical and mortuary equipment). Held: the Kota maternity annex (council
success-story post; 2024 CDF; 98% complete when posted, no cost) and three
projects named in a 2023 news report of an MP's inspection tour.

## Biggest gaps

1. **Luapula Constituency (Lunga)** — 107 well-documented ward-level rows
   are held only because Lunga publishes *submissions* and *deferred* lists
   rather than approved/implemented lists. A status-policy decision unlocks
   them.
2. **Mansa district (Bahati + Mansa Central)** — the provincial capital's
   council publishes grants/bursary lists and rich CDFC minutes, but no
   approved/implemented community project list; Mansa Central's 21 rows are
   held as pre-approval recommendations with engineers' estimates.
3. **Chifunabuli** — 42 implemented projects claimed by the council, none
   itemised anywhere findable.
4. **Chipili and Chiengi** — no council-published community project lists
   at all; only news mentions.
5. **Per-project money** — only Milenge (National Assembly), Mwansabombwe
   (press statement) and the held Mansa Central minutes publish per-project
   figures. Samfya, Kawambwa, Nchelenge, Chembe and the Mwense catalogue
   publish no per-project amounts, so most Luapula rows have blank budgets.
6. **Mwense catalogue completeness** — the catalogue claims 113 projects
   but only ~56 entries could be read plainly from its text layer; a
   page-by-page visual read of the PDF would likely recover more.
