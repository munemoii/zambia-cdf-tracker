# North-Western Province — CDF project-list coverage

Collected following the Lusaka pilot method: only rows taken plainly from officially
published lists (council/government publications, or council posts that enumerate
projects) are in `projects.csv`. Anything weaker, ambiguous or conflicting is in
`held.csv` with a reason. Facebook was not used (login-gated).

## Totals

- `projects.csv`: 128 rows, 16 sources with live rows
- `held.csv`: 110 rows (64 Kabompo + 39 Mwinilunga-2024 + 7 single reports)
- `sources.csv`: 23 sources

## Per constituency

### Chavuma — 14 rows (projects.csv)
- PLGO notification of approval dated 22 May 2025 (Chavuma Town Council site):
  10 community projects with per-project amounts, plus a separate feeder-roads
  (fuel & lubricants) section of 3 road-gravelling items — all included.
  Amounts used are the "Estimates" column (they sum to the document total
  K11,377,455.20); the document also prints higher "Recommended after Review"
  figures, which were NOT used.
- A second PLGO notification the same day approves 1 disaster project
  (rehabilitation of 1x2 CRB at Chiyeke, K569,990.75).
- Funding year: the documents do **not** explicitly name a CDF funding year
  (they are approval letters dated May 2025 under the CDF Act No. 1 of 2024).

### Ikeleng'i — 11 rows (projects.csv)
- PLGO notification of approval dated 2 Feb 2024 (Ikelenge Town Council site):
  11 community projects (labelled "Disaster") approved with amounts; a 12th item
  (contribution towards a skills centre, K1,000,000) was **deferred** and is excluded.
  Amounts used are the Estimates column (approved items sum to K15,589,200.12).
  For Muzenzi rural health post the Engineers' reviewed figure is higher
  (K2,250,000, +17%) and the notification warns extra funds will be needed — the
  estimate was still recorded as published.
- Funding year: **not** explicitly named (application dated Jan 2024, CDF Act No. 11
  of 2018; no "2024 CDF" wording).

### Kabompo — 0 rows live, 64 rows held
- Kabompo Town Council publishes an MLGRD-headed "Kabompo CDF 2024 Community
  Projects Applications" PDF listing 64+ project applications by ward with
  descriptions, sectors and sites — but it states **no approval status**, so all
  rows are held rather than guessed as approved. (The PDF's numbering duplicates
  No. 44 and No. 63 is blank; both quirks are the document's own.)

  grants and bursaries only — no community-project list found.
- Funding year: the applications document is titled 2024.

### Kasempa — 17 rows (projects.csv), 4 held
- Kasempa Town Council's own "2023 Community Projects Approved List" PDF:
  17 approved community projects with sectors and wards, no amounts published.
- The council's news pages confirm commissioning of items on that list
  (Jifumpa 1x3 CRB, maternity annex, Ngoma staff house) — not duplicated.
- Held: Lufupa 1x4 CRB (K1.7m, 2024 CDF) from a single Open Zambia news report;
  Kankolonkolo police staff housing (K466,474), Kizhiba teacher's house (K470,191)
  and a K1,050,000 medical-equipment handover, all from the secondary Chalo Chatu
  constituency page rather than an official list.
- Funding year: **explicit — 2023** (document title).

### Manyinga — 0 rows (gap)
- The only project document Manyinga Town Council publishes for 2025 is a
  **rejection notice** (14 Apr 2025): 67 proposed community projects were rejected
  for insufficient funds/priority. No approved community-project list was found on
  the council site or elsewhere. Council news mentions over 20 projects underway

### Mufumbwe — 26 rows (projects.csv)

  "Approved"/"Approved (1)"/"Approved (3)" are included; the rest were

### Mwinilunga — 12 rows (projects.csv), 39 held
- 2025: Mwinilunga Town Council's CDFC decision notice (7 Mar 2025) assesses 51
  WDC proposals and approves 5 proposals (K17,750,444 budget): a clinic in
  Kankang'a, a chief's palace, electrification of Kanyikomboshi School area,
  earth-moving equipment (5 items, recorded as 5 rows), and furniture for 3
  maternity annexes + 3 health centres (1 row). The other 46 proposals were
  NOT APPROVED (inadequate CDF allocation) and are excluded.
- 2022 (handed over Dec 2023): a council post enumerates 3 completed classroom
  blocks (Kanyihampa, Nswanakuda, Kanzhimu) — total K722,062 for all three, so no
  per-project amounts were recorded.
- Held: the council's "Shortlisted Community Projects for CDF 2024 (Final Revised)"
  PDF — it is a shortlist, not an approval, and it prints the same ~35 projects
  twice with differing figures (e.g. Mudanyama Health Centre estimate K797,448.77
  in version 1 vs K749,633.60 in version 2). All 39 rows held with the first
  version's Engineers Estimate.
- Constituency naming: the council's 2024 document says "Mwinilunga East
  Constituency"; rows are filed under the ECZ name **Mwinilunga** as instructed.
- Funding year: 2025 decision notice — **explicit (2025 CDF allocation)**;
  handover post — **explicit (2022 CDF)**; 2024 shortlist — **explicit (CDF 2024 /
  2024 CDF allocation)**.

## Cross-cutting notes

- **PLGO approval PDFs** (Chavuma, Ikeleng'i, Zambezi East, Solwezi East) are hosted
  on the respective council websites; they print two money columns (Estimates and
  Recommended after Review). The Estimates column was recorded consistently
  because it is the column that sums to each document's stated total.
- The Chavuma roads total (K3,278,013.69) is identical to the fuel figure on
  Solwezi Municipal Council's 2025 projects page. Both are recorded as published;
  the Chavuma figure is internally supported by its own 3-item breakdown, which
  sums exactly.
- Councils that publish nothing findable as an approved community-project list:
  **Manyinga** (rejections only) and **Kalumbila/Solwezi West** (lists exist on the
  downloads page but files were not retrievable in this pass). Mushindamo and
  Solwezi Municipal publish prose project updates rather than tabular lists.
- No council website refused the connection outright in this pass; the failures
  were unindexed/unexposed download links (Kalumbila) rather than blocked sites.
