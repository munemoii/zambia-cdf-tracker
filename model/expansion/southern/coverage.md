# Southern Province CDF expansion — coverage report

Collected: 30 October 2026 (research pass, published sources only), following the Lusaka pilot method.
Output: `projects.csv` — **311 project rows** across 19 of the 20 Southern Province constituencies, traced to 41 sources in `sources.csv`; `held.csv` — **78 rows** held for review (weaker sources, garbled tables, conflicts, ambiguous attribution). Chirundu and Itezhi-Tezhi are filed under Southern for this tracker, per ECZ.

**Headline gaps:** Kalomo Central has **zero** main-list rows (Kalomo Town Council publishes no project list that could be found — news and aggregates only). Monze Central has only 1 main row: Monze Town Council's CDF page links its 2024 and 2025 approved-project PDFs, but the documents could not be retrieved in this pass (the page fetch does not expose the link targets and the filenames are not indexed by search). Siavonga Town Council's Publications page lists official approved-project documents for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026, but the files likewise could not be retrieved — the single biggest recoverable gap in the province.

## Per-constituency coverage

### Monze district (Monze Town Council)

**Monze Central — 1 main row, 1 held.** The gap is retrieval, not publication: the council's CDF page (`https://www.monzecouncil.gov.zm/?page_id=2871`) links "2024 Community Projects Monze Central" and "2025 Approved Community Projects Monze Central", but neither document could be opened in this pass. What was captured: the council's 21 May 2025 approvals article (aggregates: K8.78m to complete phased 2023/2024 projects, K9.41m for new 2025 projects, K900,950 climate-resilience top-up from 2024 CDF) and one plainly stated item from it — the Mulongalwili Primary School classroom rehabilitation (2025 disaster component). Held: Chief Monze's Palace (commissioned 2025 at a stated K4.1m, but funded by a CDF/MLGRD blend, so the CDF share is not published). Council news also reports two ambulances for Moomba and Bweengwa procured centrally by MLGRD using 2023 CDF — not rowed (central procurement; Moomba's own 2024 list separately includes an ambulance purchase).

**Bweengwa — 26 main rows, 1 held.** The full 2025 approved list (26 items with wards and scope remarks; no amounts published) plus the Hamangaba Rural Health Centre solar system from the approvals article (explicitly 2024 CDF). Held: the list item "Boraks", whose meaning/category cannot be determined plainly. The council CDF page also links a 2024 Bweengwa community-projects list and a 2024 drought-response list — not retrieved (same link problem as Monze Central).

**Moomba — 25 main rows.** The full 2024 list: 3 drought-response items + 22 community projects, with wards. No amounts published. Note the printed "Competition Mwila Dip tank" is rowed as "Completion of Mwila Dip Tank" (apparent typo in the PDF, flagged here). "Conduct of Radio Programmes" is rowed as published (administration) although it is a service rather than an asset.

### Choma district (Choma Municipal Council)

**Choma — 18 main rows, 12 held.** The council publishes a proper status table for Choma Central's 2024 CDF projects (ward, description, scope, sector, estimated cost, contractor, status): 30 rows. Caveats: the figures are the council's **estimated** costs, and several rows are "additional funds" top-ups rather than full project costs. 18 rows were clean enough to publish; 11 site water-scheme rows and the Singani East staff house are held because the PDF's column layout merges site and ward (or loses the status cell). For the four water rows whose ward is attested elsewhere in the same table (Simpweze→Nakeempa, Mwapona→Simacheche, Choye→Singani, Mochipapa→Siamaambo), the ward was taken as published. The Art Centre's contractor cell is garbled in the PDF ("Bilarch…") and is left blank. Council-reported aggregates (not rowed): 156 community projects implemented in Choma Central 2022–2026.

**Mbabala — 3 main rows, 2 held.** No Mbabala project list is published. The main rows come from the council's own January 2025 spot-check article: Macha Police Station (Macha Ward, 55% complete, 2022+2023 CDF) and the Macha Community Hall water scheme and ablution block (handed over September 2024). Held: a staff house at Mapanza Clinic and a health post at Munzuma, which appear only in a secondary summary of council reporting. Council-reported aggregate (not rowed): 117 Mbabala community projects 2022–2026.

### Kazungula district (Kazungula Town Council)

**Katombola — 39 main rows, 5 held.** The strongest dataset in the province: the council's update report tabulates every project funded from the **2021, 2022 and 2023** CDF with contract amount, contractor, ward and completion percentage. Status mapping: 100% → completed; part percentages → ongoing with `completion_pct`; "still under tender"/"works yet to commence" → approved. Judgement calls: (a) the Kazungula Boarding School computer laboratory is marked **stalled** at 38% because the report says the contractor has not been on site since 28 September 2023; (b) the Lunungu maternity annex is marked **stalled** because the works were condemned and the contractor ordered to restart (no percentage published); (c) the Kabuyu Secondary completion amount (K85,905.00) sits in the report's amount column but a separate K85,000 desks contract is mentioned in the same row's comments — the amount is carried as printed and flagged here; (d) the Moomba Central secondary school is rowed as its four contract lots; note **Moomba here is a ward in Katombola**, not Monze's Moomba constituency; (e) the CDF monitoring vehicle is component `administration`, the only non-community row in the file. Held: the Silelo roofing row (no data; school used other funds), the 2023 ambulance (deferred to 2024 by MLGRD direction), and three overlapping TBA desk items.

### Livingstone district (Livingstone City Council)

**Livingstone — 25 main rows, 5 held.** The official Approved 2024 CDF Community Projects list (22 items with wards; no amounts). Four of those projects were later reported commissioned by the National Assembly's July 2025 newsletter with costs — taxi rank (K599,511.50), Kariba streetlights (K896,500) and the Malota ablution block (K472,000) matched the 2024 list exactly by name and ward, so cost + completed status were merged onto those rows (the Lusaka pilot's merge precedent). The fourth, "Phase II of the Botswana Drainage Project" (K1,250,000), was **not** merged: the 2024 item does not say Phase II, so the match is not certain — it is noted here instead. Three further rows come from the council PR manager's published updates (Kasiya 8 boreholes, 2025 CDF, contractor Buildstrong General Dealers; Linda Township road grading at 70%, K377,208.75; Maramba R-Section backbone sewer at 80%, K719,972, 2025 CDF). Held: five projects named in the council's June 2026 newsletter (13 commissioned, ~K7.5m total) whose text extraction is fragmentary with no per-project cost or ward. Not rowed: six major 2023 projects (including maternity wings at Maramba and Nakatindi clinics) reported by ZNBC in aggregate only.

### Mazabuka district (Mazabuka Municipal Council)

**Mazabuka Central — 14 main rows, 16 held.** The official Approved CDF 2026 Community Projects list (14 community projects with sectors and wards; no amounts). Items 15–16 of the same document (Presidential Constituency Energy Initiative; Secondary School Bursaries) are programme/bursary lines, not community projects, and are not rowed. The list's own sector labels are occasionally wrong (a maternity annex labelled "Education"); categories in the CSV follow the project, not the label. For 2025, the only itemised list found is a UPND Media Team release reproduced by the Zambian Observer — all 16 items are **held**, even though they look plausible, because the source is a party release rather than the council. The official route exists: the council's Jan–Mar 2026 minutes contain a "Status of 2025 Mazabuka Central CDF Community Projects" table with allocated/awarded amounts and contractors (the search index shows, e.g., item 9 awarded K316,000; item 15 allocated K1,100,000 / awarded K1,011,499.19 to KITOV Investment) — extracting that full table is the top Mazabuka follow-up.

**Magoye — 4 main rows, 5 held.** The Magoye CDFC minutes (10 Aug 2023) record 19 community projects approved under the 2023 CDF, but the published scan's OCR is heavily garbled: only about eight items are even partly legible. Three legible items are rowed (Lukuba Pre School CRB, K979,821.24; Chivuna Clinic X-ray screening room, K431,475.23; Mbaya Secondary CRB, K935,848.50); the rest are held with reasons, including a genuine conflict: the minutes list a Naleza Primary project under **2023** CDF (K759,862.40) while the National Assembly reports a 1x2 classroom block at Naleza commissioned under the **2024** CDF — both versions are held pending resolution. The Parliament item's second project, the Nziba Primary staff house (2024 CDF, commissioned 7 Apr 2025), is rowed. Also on the council site: an "Unapproved list of 2026 CDF community projects" for Magoye (four items rejected for insufficient funds) — deliberately **not** rowed, since the projects were not approved. Note: the minutes PDF's filename on the council site refers to Mazabuka Central while its indexed content is the Magoye extract; the URL in `sources.csv` is recorded verbatim as returned.

### Namwala district (Namwala Town Council)

**Namwala — 35 main rows.** The richest coverage in the province. (a) The full 2025 approved list (14 projects with wards; totals published: K21,028,457.82 overall, K17,750,444.13 community projects + K3,278,013.69 road maintenance — per-project amounts not published). (b) The council's branding article lists 21 completed projects from 2022–2024, each with its own CDF year and ward. In that table the project name and project type are separate columns; CSV names combine them in the form "1x2 Classroom Block at Chinyemu Community School". Two amounts were merged from the council's own commissioning article for the same projects (Chibiya 1x3 CRB, K939,211.31, 2024 CDF; Simanje staff ablution blocks, K500,000, 2023 CDF disaster component) — the years agree with the branding table. The CDFC minutes of 24 February 2025 (also published by the council) contain the 2025 proposed list **with cost estimates**; the approved-list article was used for the rows, and the minutes' estimates were not treated as approved amounts.

### Chirundu district (Chirundu Town Council)

### Gwembe district (Gwembe Town Council)

**Gwembe — 16 main rows, 1 held.** The council's CDF Utilisation Report 2023/2024 lists 14 approved 2023 community projects (K15,071,061.77 total) and then gives contract-level sections. The narrative bundles were unpacked only where the report itself gives an individual contract: the five-centre maternity-annex bundle (K4,010,000 combined) is rowed as the four centres with individual contracts (Chabbobboma K793,981.28, Chaamwe K827,281.22, Luumbo K857,520.20 — all stated Complete; Sinafala K741,958.39) and **Nyanga is held** (named in the bundle, no individual contract). ### Sinazongwe district (Sinazongwe Town Council)

**Sinazongwe — 45 main rows.** The council's 22 August 2025 handover article itemises exactly 45 completed projects by sector (12 health + 10 education + 19 water/sanitation + 4 infrastructure/security), and the CSV reproduces that count exactly. Names are composed in the form "facility at place" from the article's bullets. Conventions: wards are taken only from the bullet in which a project appears — the three ablution blocks, two health-post staff houses and three classroom rehabilitations are left ward-blank even where the same article places a same-named facility in a ward elsewhere, rather than carrying wards across bullets. No costs or per-project funding years are published. The article also puts total handovers 2022–2024 at 57, i.e. 12 earlier projects are not itemised anywhere found.

## Method notes

- Sources are council/government publications first; news items are rowed in the main file only where they are the National Assembly's own reporting of a single fully-identified project (Nziba staff house; Siavonga bulldozer) or a council article about a single project (Chirundu; Dundumwenzi; Mbabala spot checks). Everything else news-carried is in `held.csv`.
- Per-project amounts are never split from programme totals: bundled figures stay off the rows (Gwembe bundles, Namwala totals, Chikankata's two-schemes line is rowed as the single published line it is).
- Where a council list's sector label contradicts the project (Mazabuka), the category follows the project.
- Council websites intermittently refused connections during the pass (notably Monze and Siavonga document downloads); gaps caused by retrieval failure are stated as such, not as non-publication. Facebook was not used (login-gated).
