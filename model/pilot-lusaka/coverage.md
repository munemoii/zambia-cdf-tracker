# Lusaka Province CDF pilot — coverage report

Collected: 30 October 2026 (research pass, published sources only).
Output: `projects-lusaka.csv` — **182 project rows** across the 12 Lusaka Province constituencies, traced to 28 project-level sources in `sources-lusaka.csv` (33 sources captured in total; 5 are aggregate/duplicate sources listed at the end and referenced only in this report).

## Photos (new requirement)

- **1 of 182 projects has a verified project photo**: the Tubalange Police Post (Chilanga), from the Zed Gossip commissioning article (23 Apr 2026). The article's lead image (IMG-20260423-WA0003) heads a story whose entire subject is the commissioning of that police post at that site; `photo_url` is the direct image URL and `photo_credit` is "Zed Gossip (article by Francis Chipalo)". The same article's other two images were an advert and a Telegram promo graphic — not used. The photo was **not** applied to the second project in that article (Makeni GRZ Clinic laboratory), because a single header image cannot be shown to depict both facilities.
- Every other source page/document was checked. Council (LCC, Chongwe, Rufunsa, Luangwa, Chilanga), ZANIS and Lusaka Provincial Administration pages returned **no extractable direct image URLs** through the text-fetch tool used for this pass, so no photo could be verified to the required standard for them. The Kafue CDF newsletter PDF and the Luangwa CDFC minutes contain photographs embedded in the PDF, but no direct per-project image URL could be extracted, and the PDF itself is not an image URL — left blank rather than substituted. No generic/stock images were used anywhere.

## Per-constituency coverage

### Lusaka City Council constituencies (7)

**Matero — 14 rows.** Strongest LCC coverage: the full 2025 approved community-projects list (13 projects, K17,621,957.95 total, LCC statement via Zambia Monitor/RCV, 29–30 Jan 2025) with per-project amounts and wards, plus the Chunga Police Post from the LCC eight-police-posts release. Ambiguities: (a) the approved-list item "Completion of Mwembeshi Main Police Post" (K870,000) may be the same physical post as the "Chunga Police Post" counted as constructed in 2022–2024 — Chunga is in Mwembeshi Ward; both rows kept because the sources describe different stages/years, flagged here as a possible overlap. (b) A Lusaka Times report (12 Apr 2026) says a bridge in Muchinga Ward was commissioned at "over K1 million" alongside K2.7m grants to 113 groups — this is very likely the approved Matero Girls Secondary School bridge (K823,734.80 approved) at a higher final cost; not added as a separate row (duplicate), and the K2.7m/113-groups figure matches the 2026 empowerment aggregate below.

**Kanyama — 3 rows.** Only the three police posts (Makeni Bonaventure, Shalom, Leonard Cheelo) from the LCC release; per-project amounts were not published (only the K5,515,468.48 eight-post total — aggregate, below). Gap: LCC's own news index references further Kanyama items (Chinika Community Hall in Chinika Ward 11 "newly built, handed over"; CDF use in Kanyama) but the individual articles could not be opened with full text in this pass, so they were not rowed. Kanyama road works reported in July 2025 (Estimer, Chifundo, Lavania roads in Harry Mwaanga Nkumbula Ward 12; Chibolya Police Road; Chishala Road) and the Ross Breeders Road paving (Munkolo Ward, K800,000, contractor Kays Construction Limited, site handed over, RCV Sep 2024) were identified in the parent research pass but the underlying articles were not re-verified to row standard in this pass — listed here as leads, not rows.

**Kabwata — 4 rows.** Two police posts (Lackson Mapushi, Katungu; no individual amounts) plus two well-documented LCC items: Kabwata Clinic maternity annex at **90% completion** (completion_pct = 90, as stated) and the Mkandawire Secondary School science laboratory under construction. No amounts published for either.

**Lusaka Central — 2 rows.** Bauleni Mini Hospital: maternity wing + mortuary as one row because LCC published only a **combined K3,000,000** for the two facilities (2022/2023 CDF) — the amount cannot be split between them. A separate LCC item says mortuary construction had commenced at foundation level under the 2025 CDF; treated as the same project (possible year/amount conflict noted). The hospital's CDF wall fence (handed over, LSK) is the second row. Thin coverage otherwise — no approved-projects list for Lusaka Central was found.

**Mandevu — 1 row.** Only the Chaisa Police Post. LCC's news index references a CDF ablution block at Olympia Market and a commissioned Chaisa Skills Centre in Mandevu, but full articles were not captured in this pass — leads only. This is the weakest LCC constituency.

**Munali — 7 rows.** Kamanga Police Post; Chainda Open School ablution block (handed over); Kaunda Square Secondary School classroom block and Kalingalinga Primary School classes (both under construction per the MP's office statement); Kapwelyomba Primary 1×3 block (constructed); Gripps health post in Munali Ward 38 (almost complete); Chainda Primary 1×2 teachers' flat (set for handover). Sources are weaker here: the Munali Parliamentary Constituency Office page (mirrored on govserv.org — **social-source equivalent**: it republishes the constituency office's social-media-style posts, flagged as such) and a Zambian Observer summary of the MP's project tour. No amounts published for any Munali project. A Munali community sports facility in Ward 38 reported completed on Parliament's constituency-news list was not rowed (no dedicated article captured).

**Chawama — 3 rows.** Chawama Secondary School 1×6 double-storey block, K4,600,000, handed over Feb 2026 (Lusaka Times; note the funding is described as "2020–2024 CDF", spanning pre-increase years — budget-year ambiguity). Chifundo Road drainage system, K2,300,000, completed (2023 CDF). Lilayi Close Road, 2.2 km, K2,500,000, **85% complete** (2024 CDF; completion_pct = 85 as stated). LCC/LSK also report a newly built admission ward and maternity annex at Freedom Clinic, Lilayi Ward 4 (announced Jan 2025 as about to be commissioned) — no amount or dedicated article captured; lead only.

### Chongwe (Chongwe Municipal Council) — 41 rows

The council's 14 May 2025 release approving 2025 CDF community projects is the backbone. **Ambiguity: the release announces 38 projects totalling K20,864,557.40, but only 36 distinct items could be enumerated from the published text** (formatting may have merged two items). 35 of them are rowed as `approved` with wards where stated; the 36th — the Ngwerere mortuary — is rowed separately from the ZANIS construction report (K992,000, in progress, Kapwayambale Ward), which is the same project at implementation stage (duplicate across sources; one row kept, implementation source used). No per-project amounts in the approved list — only the K20.86m total (aggregate). Five completed classroom blocks from the council's "Impact of CDF on Education" article are also rowed; note Kalimasenga Primary appears in both that article (block constructed) and the 2025 list (a further phase-1 block) — kept as separate rows, possible phase overlap flagged. Aggregates: 2024 CDF — K16.1m received (53% of K30.6m), K15.6m spent; K16.6m allocated to community projects including K4.6m road fuel, K1m tractor, K3m Chongwe Modern Market contribution, K1m REA electrification, K3.2m for 2,500 desks; skills K3.6m/333 students, boarding bursaries K1,295,560/664 pupils, grants K2.2m/105 groups, loans K3.3m/19 of 21 beneficiaries (LSK, `chongwe-2024-receipts`). Desk-count discrepancy: council news (Sep 2026) says 2,000 desks procured; ZANIS (8 Oct 2026) says Mikango Secondary's 100 desks are part of **3,000** procured — unresolved, aggregates only.

### Kafue (Kafue Town Council) — 33 rows

The council's May 2024 CDF newsletter (official PDF) is the richest single source in the pilot: the full 2022 project update table (18 rows; 17 at 100% — "works completed and handed over" — and the Mulangwa Primary project still in progress with no percentage stated) and the 2023 approved-projects list. Judgement calls: (a) the newsletter narrative's Kafue Main Police Station (K2.4m, handed over Nov 2023) is the same project as the 2022 table's "Police Station in Kafue Town (CBD)" — merged into one row carrying the K2,400,000 figure and completed status; (b) the 2023 road programme is rowed per ward (6 rows) from the approved list/progress table as one programme, not double-counted with the K3,000,000 roads set-aside (aggregate); (c) "Procured" in the source is normalised to `completed` for the water bowser and the 2,910 desks. One extra row: Mahopo Health Post ablution block + water reticulation (Chisankane Ward — spelled "Chisakane" in the Observer report), handed over under 2023 CDF. Aggregates from the newsletter: 2022 loans K2,389,800/26 beneficiaries, grants K1,917,690/142 beneficiaries; 3,000 desks procured with 2022 CDF handed over Dec 2022 (the motor grader unveiled the same day is likely the table's grader row — duplicate noted); district total reported elsewhere: 70 CDF projects 2022–2024, 46 completed. The newsletter's classroom narrative also names Shantumbu Secondary School among blocks built — the table rows Shantumbu Police Post instead; only table items were rowed.

### Chilanga (Chilanga Town Council) — 17 rows

Good spread: Namalombwe Primary 1×3 block (Nyemba Ward, K893,673.27, ZANIS); Game Clinic 12KVA solar backup (Chilanga Ward, installed 18 Aug 2025 — funded under the **Disaster Component** of the 2025 CDF, which is not one of the CSV's component values, so component is left blank); Tubalange Police Post (K1,486,295.67, commissioned, **with photo**) and Makeni GRZ Clinic laboratory (commissioned, no amount) — Zed Gossip is a low-tier outlet; figures should be re-verified against a council source when one appears. Chinyanja Market Shelter phase 1 (K1,613,062.04) is rowed from the official CDFC minutes appraisal table as `approved`; the March 2026 handover report (All Zim News) says it was handed over together with the New Farms maternity annex (K1,600,000, Human Service Trust Hospice) and Westwood Primary electrification (K582,000) — those two are rowed as completed from that report, but **the aggregator's URL could not be captured verbatim (the search tool flagged the destination unavailable), so `chilanga-handover-2026` has a blank url in sources-lusaka.csv** — the one source-link gap in the pilot. The minutes also record the Lukamantano feeding shelter as **stalled** (contractor Beyun Engineering Limited abandoned the works; K16,317.31 paid of the K60,000 approved amount — the row carries the approved K60,000) and a radiological unit (Chilanga Ward, K1,081,994.15, appraised viable/recommended → approved), and they discuss completing a maternity wing in New Farms — probably the same facility later handed over in 2026; only the handover row was kept. Eight completed projects from the Minister's handover (reported via Africa Press/ZANIS) are rowed without amounts. Aggregates: K21,028,457.83 invested in 24 CDF projects district-wide (Council Chairperson, Mar 2026); since 2022 — 245 secondary bursary pupils, 4,000+ desks, six 1×3 classroom blocks (council, Oct 2025); 2023 loans "over K3 million" to 17 cooperatives and grants K2.1m to 55 clubs (handover report).

### Rufunsa (Rufunsa Town Council) — 12 rows

Low-bed truck (K2,861,082.00, 2024 CDF, handed over 23 Dec 2025 — Parliament); three 2023 CDF education handovers with amounts (Kankumba 1×2 science laboratory K821,049.16; Chinzete and Fresh Air 1×3 blocks K950,000.00 each — ward spellings vary between "Kankumba/Kankumbi/Kakumba" in the same article; standardised to Kankumba Ward); seven feeder roads launched under CDF (council; "over K3 million allocated to road works" is an aggregate — no per-road amounts); one semi-detached health staff house handed over at "over K935,000" (row carries 935000; the "over" qualifier is noted here — location/ward not stated). Not rowed: the 22 km Sinjela feeder road was graded using CDF-**purchased equipment** rather than as a CDF-funded road project (equipment-use activity); ZANIS reporting of a stalled/abandoned district police station (and stalled District Administration and Civic Centre) at a DDCC meeting could not be re-verified to row standard in this pass — an important lead, because Rufunsa currently has no `stalled` row. The council also published a 2026 community-projects **not-approved** list (insufficient funds) — applications, not projects; not rowed.

### Feira (Luangwa Town Council) — 45 rows

The deepest constituency in the pilot, thanks to the council's own 2022 project database (26 community projects; approved amounts = engineers' estimates in every row; total K13,916,144.81) plus the July 2025 CDFC minutes (12 community projects funded by a K1,900,000 skills-bursaries variation — the table as published sums to K2,144,000, i.e. more than the variation; arithmetic discrepancy in the source, noted here) plus 7 contracts handed over under the 2025 CDF (status `funded` = contracts awarded/handed over to contractors; no amounts published). Status mapping from the database's free-text comments: "Done" → completed; progress wording ("roofing", "foundation level", mobilisation) → in_progress; "Procurement Level"/"Reprocurement stage" → funded; the utility vehicle's status cell reads "MLGRD" (not a progress status) → unknown. **Duplicate with conflicting amounts:** the Janeiro foot bridge appears in the 2022 database at K412,300.50 with a blank status, and Parliament reports it handed over 2 Feb 2025 by contractor **WEGOS General Dealers** at **K653,775.89** (`parliament-feira-bridge`). Per the duplicate rule the council-database row was kept (contractor therefore blank in the CSV); the completion and the ~K241k cost variance are recorded here. Other ambiguities: database approval dates (July 2023) postdate the 2022 funding year; two rows are numbered "2" in the original; ward spellings as published (M'kaliva vs Mkaliva, Lunya, Mankhokwe); the July 2025 minutes' Undi incinerator top-up (K54,000) likely relates to the Chiriwe health post row but is a distinct funded item, kept separate; the Dec 2025 minutes' 2025 empowerment-loans total (K3,736,935.62 to 40 beneficiaries) and the 2026 proposed-projects table are aggregates/proposals, not rows. The 2025 contracts list's "single staff house at Mukando" may overlap the database's Mukando Village pre-school area items but is a different named project; kept separate.

## Biggest gaps

1. **LCC publishes almost no consolidated project lists.** Only Matero has a full approved list with amounts. The other six LCC constituencies are reconstructed from one-off council news items — Mandevu (1 row), Lusaka Central (2) and Kanyama (3) are certainly undercounts of what CDF has funded there.
2. **Per-project amounts are missing for most LCC, Chongwe, Rufunsa and Luangwa-2025 rows** — councils publish totals and counts, not project budgets (exceptions: Matero list, Feira database, Kafue newsletter narrative items, and individual ZANIS/LSK stories).
3. **Photos: only 1 verifiable project photo** could be captured under the "demonstrably depicts that project + direct image URL" rule (see Photos section). A browser-based pass over the council Facebook pages and news galleries would likely raise this substantially.
4. **No social-source rows were needed** except the Munali constituency-office page (govserv mirror of the office's posts, flagged above). Council Facebook pages were not systematically mined in this pass.
5. **Chawama's pre-2022 funding description** ("2020–2024 CDF" for the K4.6m block) and several multi-year projects (Bauleni) blur budget years; the CSV has no budget-year column, so year attribution lives in the source documents, not the rows.
6. One source (`chilanga-handover-2026`, All Zim News) has a **blank URL** — destination flagged unavailable by the search tool on three attempts; title, publisher and date are recorded so it can be re-linked later.
